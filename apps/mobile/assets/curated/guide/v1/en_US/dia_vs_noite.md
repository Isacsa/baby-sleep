**During the day:**
• Plenty of natural light
• Normal interactions, talking, playing
• Everyday sounds (no need for total silence)
• Naps can be in a brighter environment

**During the night:**
• Low light or dark environment
• Minimal, calm interactions
• Soft voice, slow movements
• Avoid excessive stimulation during feeds/changes

**Why it matters:**
The baby's circadian rhythm develops in the first weeks of life. These differences help the body understand when it's day and when it's night.

**Practical tip:**
It doesn't have to be perfect. Small consistencies over time are more important than doing everything "right" on a single day.
