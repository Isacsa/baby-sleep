**ABCs of Safe Sleep:**
• **A**lone — no pillows, loose blankets, or toys
• **B**ack — always on their back to sleep
• **C**rib — firm, flat surface

**Checklist (first 12 months):**
☐ Baby sleeps on back
☐ Firm, flat mattress
☐ Fitted sheet
☐ No pillows, loose blankets or toys
☐ Comfortable temperature (not too warm)
☐ Smoke-free room
☐ Room-sharing (not bed-sharing) recommended until 6–12 months

**After 12 months:**
Safe sleep recommendations can be adjusted. Consult your pediatrician if in doubt.

**Why it matters:**
These recommendations significantly reduce the risk of problems during sleep.
