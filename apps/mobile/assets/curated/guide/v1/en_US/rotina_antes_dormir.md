**What is a sleep routine?**
A short, predictable sequence of calm activities before sleep. It helps the baby understand that it's time to wind down.

**Simple example (2–4 steps):**
1. Bath or wash
2. Put on pajamas
3. Soft song or short story
4. Low light and goodnight

**Tips:**
• Keep the same order every day
• Start 20–30 minutes before bedtime
• Do it in the same place (bedroom or quiet area)
• Less is more — long routines can tire more than relax

**What to avoid:**
• Screens or stimulating toys
• Active games or lots of laughter
• Too much talking or "negotiations"

**Don't worry if:**
Some days don't go well. What matters is consistency over time, not perfection every night.
