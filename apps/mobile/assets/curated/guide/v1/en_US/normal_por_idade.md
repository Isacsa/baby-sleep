**0–3 months (Newborn)**
• Total sleep: 14–17 hours per day
• Short blocks (2–4 hours), day and night
• High variability is completely normal
• The circadian rhythm is still developing

**4–12 months**
• Total sleep: 12–16 hours (including naps)
• Longer night blocks start to appear
• 2–3 naps during the day
• Regressions are common at 4, 6 and 9 months

**12–24 months**
• Total sleep: 11–14 hours (including naps)
• Typically 1–2 naps
• May resist bedtime (testing limits)
• Transition to 1 nap usually happens at 15–18 months

Every baby is unique. These are typical ranges, not rigid rules.
