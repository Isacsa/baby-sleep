**Trust your instinct.**
If something concerns you, talk to your pediatrician. There are no "silly" questions.

**Signs that need medical attention:**
• Irregular breathing or long pauses
• Changed skin color (bluish or very pale)
• Fever in babies < 3 months
• Difficulty waking up or extreme lethargy
• Persistent refusal to feed
• Inconsolable crying different from usual

**About sleep itself:**
• Sudden changes in pattern without obvious cause
• Consistent snoring or noisy breathing
• Frequent panic awakenings (different from normal nightmares)

**What is NOT necessarily worrying:**
• Normal variability in sleep pattern
• Difficult nights during regressions or teething
• Preference for falling asleep in arms (very common)

**Remember:**
This guide is informational. It does not replace personalized medical advice.
